## Status vocabulary

- **IMPLEMENTED** — source or workflow exists; this alone is not evidence that it works in the target environment.
- **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED** — an architectural slice exists, but one or more acceptance cases are missing.
- **VERIFIED AT REVISION** — the linked test or independent check passed against the exact SHA shown in the evidence.
- **NOT VERIFIED** — implementation may exist, but no adequate evidence has been retained for the claim.
- **BLOCKED** — a known failure, security finding, environment discrepancy, or required approval prevents acceptance.
